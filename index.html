Questions

Will Basso change my voice?

Basso gives you a structured daily practice and a weekly measurement so you can see your own trend. Results vary from person to person and depend on how consistently you practice. It is a training tool, not a medical device.

Is it free?

You can try it free for 7 days. After that a subscription is required, and the price shown in the store before you subscribe depends on your country.

Where does my audio go?

Your recordings are analyzed on your device. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.

It is coming soon. For now Basso is available on Google Play.
